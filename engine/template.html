      Safety

      Accessibility

      • Every panel has a written description for screen readers, and all words are real text (not part of the picture).
      • Motion is optional and follows your device's reduced-motion setting. No flashing, no autoplay sound.
      • The story works fully with sound off and motion off. Text size can go up to extra large.
      • Storybook mode: tap the right or left edge, swipe, use the ◀ Back / Next ▶ buttons, or press ← and →. Tapping characters and objects is an optional extra; nothing in the story depends on it.

      Privacy

      No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no network calls for the story. Only the reading position and settings are saved, in this browser on this device.
